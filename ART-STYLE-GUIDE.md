## Character Depiction Rules

### Prophets (STRICTEST)
- **NEVER** show face, front view, or detailed features
- Always depicted as: radiant silhouette, figure bathed in golden light, seen from far away, or from behind
- Clothing: flowing white or cream robes, no specific cultural markers
- Surrounded by divine light (warm gold glow)

### Companions & Historical Figures
- Similar to prophets: faces obscured by light or shown from behind
- Can show more body detail (clothing, posture, hands)
- Distinguishing features through clothing color/style, not facial features

### Children (Story Characters)
- Can show more body detail than prophets
- Face visible with big expressive cartoon eyes, round cheerful features, diverse skin tones
- Expressive through both facial expressions and body language
- Clothing: modest, colorful, culturally diverse Muslim attire

### Animals
- Can be depicted normally with full detail
- Friendly, warm expressions
- Slightly stylized (children's book aesthetic)

### Nature & Architecture
- Full detail encouraged
- Mosques: beautiful, diverse architectural styles (not just one type)
- Landscapes: lush, inviting, magical quality
- Stars, moon, sky: prominent, wonder-inducing

## Scene Composition Tips

### DO:
- Wide shots showing landscapes with small figures
- Over-the-shoulder perspectives
- Silhouettes against dramatic skies (sunset, starry night)
- Close-ups of hands (praying, sharing food, holding objects)
- Focus on environment with figures as part of the scene
- Warm interior scenes (candlelit rooms, cozy spaces)

### DON'T:
- Portrait-style close-ups of any human
- Front-facing human characters
- Scary or dark imagery (even for conflict scenes)
- Violent scenes (battles shown as distant/abstract)
- Western-centric architecture or clothing exclusively

## Page Layout Guidance

### Cover Images
- Central iconic element (the story's key symbol)
- Title space at top or bottom
- Rich, inviting colors that make children want to tap/read
- Slightly more vibrant than interior pages

### Interior Pages
- Scene fills the frame (full-bleed illustration)
- Leave visual "breathing room" for text overlay
- Consistent lighting direction within a story
- Progressive visual arc (start warm → build → gentle resolution)

### Last Pages
- Extra calm and peaceful
- Moonlight, stars, sleeping imagery
- Warm, cozy tones
- Perfect for transitioning to sleep
