About Islamic Bedtime Stories for Kids

Islamic bedtime stories help Muslim children connect with their faith through the timeless art of storytelling. At NoorBedtime, we bring the beauty of the Quran, the wisdom of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), and the courage of Islamic heroes to life through short, illustrated stories designed for the moments before sleep.

Our library of Muslim kids stories spans ages 3 to 12, covering prophet stories like the patience of Yusuf, the bravery of Ibrahim, and the compassion of Muhammad — alongside everyday tales of kindness, honesty, and gratitude rooted in Quranic teachings. Each story is sourced from authentic Islamic texts including Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, and classical Sirah literature.

Unlike generic children's story apps, NoorBedtime is built exclusively for Muslim families who want their children to hear stories that reflect their values. Our illustrations follow Islamic art traditions — no faces of prophets are depicted — creating a unique, respectful visual experience. Whether you're looking for Quran stories for kids, Islamic children's books online, or halal bedtime content, NoorBedtime is the home your family has been waiting for.
