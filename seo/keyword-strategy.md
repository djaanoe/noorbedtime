## Competitor Keyword Gaps

### Miraj Stories Analysis
**Strengths**: Strong in prophet stories, comprehensive Quran coverage
**Gaps NoorBedtime can fill**:
- Long-tail targeting specific age groups (2-4, 5-7, 8-10)
- Companion stories (Sahaba) focus
- Character building/values-oriented storytelling
- Modern app experience with interactive elements
- Parenting resources and guides
- Story personalization by character and theme

### Noor Kids Analysis
**Strengths**: Interactive element, subscription model
**Gaps NoorBedtime can fill**:
- Deep keyword targeting in character building stories
- SEO content marketing (blog posts)
- Specific Islamic values/lessons framing
- Mobile-first story design SEO
- Schema markup for stories (Book type)
- Community-focused story recommendations

### General Competitors Gap Analysis
**Underexploited Keywords**:
- "Islamic stories for [specific age group]" variations
- "Muslim [value] stories" (honesty, patience, kindness, etc.)
- Long-form Quranic story explanations
- Islamic practice stories (salah, Ramadan, etc.)
- Local cultural Islamic stories

**Opportunity**: Build authority in age-specific Islamic story content while competitors focus on broad "Islamic stories" terms.
